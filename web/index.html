Passphrase Deck

Each deal makes one secret in two formats. Use the readable format or the compact format for the same account. Do not use both for different accounts.

Your passphrase (one secret, two formats)

Readable

Words with spaces. Easy to read and type.

Hidden

Compact

The same secret without spaces. Easy to paste.

Hidden

Values stay in page memory only. A reload discards them. The page does not clear the clipboard.

Settings

Both formats meet the length range and the entropy floor. Punctuation and digits are fixed by the design.
